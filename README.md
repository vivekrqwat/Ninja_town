Ninja Town 🥷🪙A fast-paced, 2D arcade evasion game built with the PhaserJS framework. In Ninja Town, speed, agility, and quick reflexes are your best weapons. Avoid relentless enemies, navigate the urban rooftops, and collect as many coins as possible before you get caught!🎮 Game OverviewYou are a stealthy ninja trapped in a hostile town. Enemies are actively hunting you down. Your mission is simple: survive and get rich.The Goal: Escape the pursuing enemies while gathering scattered gold coins.The Challenge: The longer you survive, the more aggressive the pursuit becomes.✨ FeaturesFluid Movement: Smooth, responsive physics-based ninja controls (running, jumping, and dodging).Dynamic Enemy AI: Enemies that track your movements to corner you.Score System: Track your collected coins and try to beat your personal high score.Retro Visuals: Crisp 2D sprite artwork and animations tailored for a classic arcade feel.Web-Based: Play instantly in any modern browser without installations.🛠️ Built WithPhaser 3 - The fast, free, and fun HTML5 game framework.JavaScript (ES6+)HTML5 & CSS3
